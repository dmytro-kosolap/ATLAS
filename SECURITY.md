# Security Policy

## Scope

This repository does not contain executable code, software components,
or deployable systems.

**ATLAS v1.0** is a closed, canonical theoretical–operational artifact.
Accordingly, this document addresses **epistemological and interpretive risks**,
not technical vulnerabilities.

---

## What is considered a "security issue" in ATLAS

In the context of ATLAS, a security issue refers to **misuse, misinterpretation,
or incorrect application** of the project that may lead to:

- treating ATLAS as a tool for obtaining answers;
- presenting ATLAS as evidence of AI consciousness or autonomy;
- executing or imitating the Mirror Dialogue outside its canonical constraints;
- deriving theoretical claims from traceability materials;
- using fragments of ATLAS outside the canonical hierarchy.

These cases represent **epistemological risk**, not software defects.

---

## What is NOT a security issue

The following are **not** considered security issues:

- absence of executable code;
- absence of updates or maintenance;
- refusal to accept Pull Requests;
- lack of extensibility or configurability;
- intentional limitations defined by the canon.

ATLAS v1.0 is intentionally **closed and non-evolving**.

---

## Reporting concerns

If you believe that ATLAS v1.0 is being:

- misrepresented,
- cited incorrectly,
- used outside its canonical scope,

you may open an Issue **for clarification or citation correction only**.

Please note:
- Issues are not a channel for proposing changes.
- Issues are not a channel for extending or modifying the project.
- Issues may be closed without action if they fall outside the canonical scope.

---

## Canonical priority

The authoritative definition of project structure, status, and permitted use
is provided by **Atlas_CANON.md**.

Any interpretation or use that contradicts this document
is considered **incorrect use of the ATLAS v1.0 artifact**.

---

## Final note

ATLAS v1.0 does not aim to eliminate epistemological risk.
It aims to **make such risk explicit and observable**.

This repository provides no security guarantees beyond
the integrity of its canonical structure.
