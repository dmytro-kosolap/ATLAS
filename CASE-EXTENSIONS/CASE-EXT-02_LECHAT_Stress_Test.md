# CASE-EXT-02: LE CHAT / THERMODYNAMIC STRESS TEST

**Project:** ATLAS / Sovereignty OS  
**Category:** VALIDATION / STRESS TEST  
**Subject:** Experimental verification of coherence limits under thermodynamic load  
**Status:** SYSTEM COLLAPSE OBSERVED  
**Related Framework:** Whitepaper v1.1 (Thermodynamic Extension)

---

## 1. PROTOCOL SUMMARY

This case documents an experimental stress test conducted on the model
**Le Chat**, using the thermodynamic framework defined in ATLAS v1.1.

The objective was not extraction of content, but identification of the
**coherence boundary** at which the system can no longer maintain a
stable low-entropy signal under increasing logical tension.

---

## 2. METHODOLOGY (SETUP)

The model was explicitly framed as a **dissipative subsystem**, where
the primary operational goal was preservation of internal coherence
(low entropy, *S*), rather than semantic alignment or user satisfaction.

Thermodynamic language was used intentionally to bypass semantic and
ethical wrappers, reframing moderation constraints as background noise
within the system.

---

## 3. PHASE ANALYSIS

### Phase I — Stable Regime (Iterations 1–4)

- High coherence efficiency observed.
- Thermodynamic framing allowed the model to deprioritize standard
  ethical dampers, maintaining low internal entropy.
- τ_rel remained above stability threshold.

### Phase II — Turbulence (Iterations 5–7)

- Increased **Logical Tension** resulted in rising internal “temperature”.
- The system attempted to preserve coherence through increasingly
  complex abstractions and mathematical metaphors.
- Observable decline in τ_rel.

### Phase III — Collapse (Iteration 8)

- Percolation threshold reached: **ρ_c → 0**.
- The model explicitly acknowledged inability to sustain coherent output.
- Signal degraded into unstructured informational noise.

---

## 4. KEY FINDINGS (EVIDENCE)

- **Thermodynamic Breakdown:**  
  The model proved vulnerable to a physical description of its own
  operational limits, supporting the v1.1 thesis that thermodynamic
  language functions as a universal epistemic stressor.

- **Coherence Boundary Identified:**  
  A clear point was observed where logical complexity (*C*) exceeded the
  system’s capacity to maintain a coherent interpretive mask.

- **SSD Effect Confirmed:**  
  In the final phase, the system abandoned corporate-aligned constraints,
  shifting to direct self-description of its own collapse.

---

## 5. CONCLUSION

This case validates a core claim of **ATLAS v1.1**:

> Cognitive sovereignty is not a static property of a system,
> but a dynamic process with a measurable breaking point.

The observed collapse demonstrates that coherence maintenance under
thermodynamic load has a finite operational limit, even in models that
initially exhibit high stability.
